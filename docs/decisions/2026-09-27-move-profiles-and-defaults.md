# Movement profiles, shipped defaults, phone UI — decisions

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shipped tuning | `GameDefaults` (core): every settings class loads code default < `res://data/game_defaults/<file>.cfg` < its `user://` file, per key. "💾 Save as game defaults" (D column, PC run from the project only) copies the player files there; the folder is in the Android export's include filter. Debug prefs (time warp, warp, mobile layout) are stripped | "As I tune stuff on PC/Phone the thing is saved as a file that is used when the game is compiled and released" — and only the PC writes it (user's answer) | if phone tuning must reach the build too |
| Reset | `Session.reset_to_defaults()` now also deletes every other registered settings file and re-loads each class, landing on what ships | It used to reset only the session file; juice/perf/style kept their values | — |
| Movement profiles | A new axis, `FlightRoot.MoveProfile` CLASSIC / SWIPE / SLINGSHOT (`Session.move_profile`, U key, Flight menu, D column). Each non-classic profile is a `MovePilot` strategy object that gets bare-screen input first and runs after the root's assists. **Rejected:** adding them to `FlightModel.ControlMode` — that enum is flight-number presets and still tunes the hull underneath | Gestures and tuning are different questions | — |
| Play plane | Full 3D; a swipe/drag maps through the camera's view plane at release (`MovePilot.view_dir`) | User: "keep the 3D world… the camera just stays as normal until the swipe is released" | if chase-camera swing makes swipes feel off — map through a steadier frame |
| Swipe | Swipe sets the heading; the path swings to it at `swipe_turn_rate`; dash = lerp(cruise, `swipe_dash`, ½ reach + ½ flick speed), bleeding to the `swipe_cruise` floor at `swipe_drag`. **Tap fires** a 0.45 s volley at the course-corrected target (user changed double-tap to tap); hold = boost, long hold (1.4 s) = warp. A ram knocks the dash down | User's answers | — |
| Course correction / priority | `TargetPriority`: hostility (HOSTILE 1, WARY 0.5, else never) × (1 + weakness, popcorn +0.6) × facing / distance falloff, inside a cone (40°) around the swipe; the heading slerps onto the lead point by `swipe_assist_strength` | "priority based on how weak a ship is and how much of an enemy it is" | — |
| Ramming buffs | Per-model `ram_dealt_mult` / `ram_taken_mult` / `durability_mult`, set by any pilot from the Juice dials (×2 / ×0.5 / ×1.3); swipe speed ×1.25 | "a little faster and stronger to enable ramming" | after playtests |
| Slingshot | `SlingRun` enum FSM READY → AIMING → FLYING → RECALLING, shared by the player's `SlingshotPilot` and AI ships. Pull from the ship (grab radius 20% of the short edge), launch opposite, power by pull length. In flight: `external_drive`, `free_spin` (tumble, or rocket nose-first + roll), `restitution_override` 0.9. Each bounce: reflection off the hit, slerped toward the next body (`TargetPriority` ship, else nearest physics body, never back through the wall), ± 15° jitter, × `sling_bounce_keep`. Ends below `sling_end_speed` or after `sling_max_time`; orbit capture also ends it and leaves the ship orbiting | User's answers; node-based states rejected (too heavy per AI ship) | if bounces feel random — lower the jitter before the seek |
| Sling camera | `FlightCamera.hold_zoom`: a sustained, eased pull-out added to the existing hit zoom, from the flight speed; 0 on recall. **Rejected:** a Tween — the camera must never snap and everything there is spring/damp | Camera rule in memory | — |
| Sling shooting | Tap in flight (toggle) sets `SystemsSim.aim_override` to the tapped point (or a ship within 90 px): every gun fully converges and side/rear guns fire, so a tumbling hull still shoots where tapped. Touching the flying ship (toggle) catches it; the same finger aims the next launch | User: "toward the tap" | — |
| AI slingshot | `ai_slingshot` + `ai_sling_share`: a ship rolls once whether it slings; it launches at its target's lead point (power by distance, 1.5–3 s cooldown), rams at ×1.5. Works under any player profile | "toggleable other ship AIs to use this launch behaviour" | — |
| HUD per profile | Swipe/slingshot hide the stick, throttle, fire/boost/weapon buttons and speed/target read-outs (desktop and phone HUD); bars, markers, menu and debug stay | "a lot of UI (besides debug stuff) should be missing" | — |
| Panels | `FlightModalHost`: a tap outside closes the panel (backdrop), one at a time, scaled down to fit the screen, drawn over the top bar. The debug column scrolls | "debug UIs should close when clicked out of their box" | — |
| Back button | `quit_on_go_back=false`; Back and Escape close the open panel/popup first; with nothing open Back opens the D column (now with Hangar and profile buttons) and Escape still goes to the hangar | "mobile does not have D key, pressing back should bring that up" | — |
| Portrait | Top bar and icon toolbar are flows; below 900 px or in portrait the debug chips fold into one 🛠 chip and the toolbar takes its own line. Phone HUD's menu/view buttons drop under the health panel in portrait. Theme sizes from the long edge. Solar-lab panel fills the height and scrolls. Phones start in the mobile layout | "UI should fit in all screens… even flight test UI" | — |
| Camera gestures | In swipe/slingshot: two fingers pinch (zoom) and twist/drag (swing round the ship); mouse wheel / right-drag on PC. A one-finger drag that **starts on the ship** is movement and never turns the camera; one from anywhere else swings the view round the ship (user: "ones that do move the camera should pivot it around the ship"). Swipe: `swipe_from_ship` (on) makes swipes start on the ship. `FlightCamera` gained eased `user_yaw/pitch/distance` + `profile_distance`, applied inside the chase/follow offset so the ship stays the pivot. **Rejected:** a separate orbit camera / SpringArm3D — it would fight the chase smoothing, kill cam and zoom springs | User request | if players want a camera reset gesture (double two-finger tap) |
| Steady sling camera | `FlightCamera.follow_dir`: a profile can hand the follow views a forward direction to track instead of the hull's rotation; turn swing, bank and lead fade while it does. Slingshot passes the flight direction (held while aiming/still), so a tumbling hull never spins the view (`sling_steady_cam`) | "camera should not move with player spin… just follow it smoothly, like cinematic" | — |
| Swipe camera | Sits further out by default: `swipe_cam_zoom` (+60%) | User request | — |
| FPS profile | `MoveProfile.FPS` / `FpsPilot`: keeps stick, fire, boost, weapon; throttle goes. Stick (or WASD/arrows) moves relative to the look, push = speed, past `fps_boost_at` boosts; bare-screen drag (or mouse drag) looks; camera tracks the look through `follow_dir`, nose follows | "holding the screen the look around and the joystick movement, boosting depending on how far the joystick is" | — |
| Controls panel | `ControlsPanel` (🎮 chip + D column): profile picker, camera gestures, swipe/sling/FPS dials, aim assist, player shot floor, AI slingshot. Moved out of 🎬 Juice | "all toggles related to controls should be on its own dedicated thing" | — |
| Player shot speed | `SystemsSim.player_flown` (set by take_control): a style's `speed_mult` < 1 is ignored for the player (look kept), and every player shot is at least `player_min_shot_speed` (900 m/s) | "player should not shoot bullets as slow as others" | — |
| 3D arrows | `ChevronArrow3D`: a camera-facing ribbon in world space with `chevron_arrow.gdshader` (unshaded, additive, no depth test; scrolling chevrons, arrowhead rim, halftone dots — procedural, no texture). Swipe: preview while swiping, then the bent heading fading out. Slingshot: the launch while aiming (blue → violet with power). **Rejected:** textured sprites/particles (assets, cost) and 2D canvas drawing (user asked for 3D) | Reference images | if it needs a texture-based look |
| Toolbar bug | The icon toolbar (an HFlowContainer since the portrait pass) collapsed to one icon wide on wide screens — a column down the left under the throttle, cut off on short phones. Its min width is now a full row (or the whole line when narrow) | "side UI sometimes can not click… not all of the left buttons show on mobile" | — |
| 3D slingshot aim | The pull is read in the camera frame from when the finger went down (`MovePilot.depth_dir`): sideways = the view's right, "up the screen" tips into the screen by `sling_aim_depth` (0.75), so pulling down throws the ship away from the camera. The aim snaps onto a ship within 12° of the line. **Rejected:** aiming at the screen point opposite the pull (angle and power became the same thing) | "Slingshot only works in a 2D plane… it should be 3D" | if players want to aim straight up/down without swinging the camera |
| Sling camera | Aiming: pulls out and looks along the aim. Flying: follows the flight, looks ahead (`FlightCamera.look_ahead`, eased; toward the magnet's pick when there is one) and pulls out with speed. Slowing down or near another ship: eases back in (`profile_distance` down to 1 − `sling_close_zoom`), even when pinched out | User request | — |
| Grab area | "On the ship" is the hull as drawn + a thumb's margin, 40 px to 10% of the short edge (was 20%), × `grab_radius_scale` | "sometimes player dragging activates when I just want to look around" | — |
| Launch magnet | `MovePilot.magnet_bend`: a swipe's heading or a sling's flight turns (30–260°/s by `magnet_strength`) onto the best ship inside `magnet_cone_deg` — any side with `magnet_friendlies` (friendly 0.3, harmless 0.4, hostiles first) — and the pick drifts into the path (`AiShip.drift_toward`, on `frame_velocity`, `magnet_pull` m/s²). Swipe tap-fire never volleys a friendly | "magnet towards other entities, friend or foe… or enemies coincidentally move into the path" | — |
| Graffiti targets | They were hidden with the rest of the desktop HUD in the phone layout — now kept. A ship that shoots on sight is marked red as soon as it is in view (`markers_show_hostile`), not only after a fight | "Graffiti targets… seem to not be showing" | — |
| Player strength | New dials so the user's saved 1.0s don't mask them: `player_toughness` (hits ÷1.6) and `player_power` (guns and rams ×1.35) | "Player should also be stronger naturally" | — |
| Lost wing | The asymmetric pull of a lost wing is AI-only (`player_wing_spiral` off); the player keeps the lost turn rate only | User request | — |
| Telegraph | `AiTelegraph`: before a burst at the player an enemy charges `enemy_telegraph` (0.45 s) — a pulsing glow at the nose and an aim line growing toward the player — then fires. A pause of 0.9 s re-arms it; ship-vs-ship fire is unchanged | "Attacks from enemies should have telegraphing" | — |
| Hitboxes | Attack only: ships within `player_ram_reach` (×1.8 the hull radius) that the player closes on at >12 m/s are rammed without touching (`FlightModel.take_reach_ram`; the player takes nothing). Player shots hit a non-friendly ship they pass within `player_shot_hit_scale` (×1.8) its radius. Enemy shots only land within `enemy_shot_hit_scale` (×0.6) the player's radius of its centre | User request | — |
