# Bullet-hell & fairness batch — decisions

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enemy fire variety | Each AI ship rolls a firing *style* at spawn (`AiFirePatterns`: stream, burst, fan, orbs, spiral, sniper, classic) plus its own cadence (×0.6–1.6). A style reshapes the shot (speed, size, life, fan/spiral, aim wobble), its burst/rest rhythm, and its sound spacing and pitch. Kept separate from the weapon table: the weapon is *what* fires, the style is *how this ship uses it* | "Bigger and more diverse cooldown… a bullet hell vibe… slow bullets and shots in streams". A style dimension multiplies variety without touching the tuned weapon table | if fights read as noise — lower `ai_fire_variety` before removing styles |
| Fans come from ≤ 2 guns | A fan/spiral volley uses at most two of a hull's muzzles | A 30-gun hull fanning from every barrel put 160 orbs on screen and took the player 399 → 171 hull in 3 s — a wall, not a pattern | — |
| Real enemy projectiles | Enemy shots hit the player only by touching the hull (ray + an orb's graze radius); the old per-volley dice roll is the fallback with the toggle off. Enemy hitscan guns become travelling shots. Damage = weapon damage × `enemy_shot_damage_scale` (0.22) | Slow bullets are pointless unless dodging them works | if the player takes too little/much — the scale dial first |
| Off-screen mercy (cheat) | A hit from a ship outside the camera frustum counts with `offscreen_hit_chance` (15%), at most `offscreen_hit_budget` (2) per 10 s. Decided at hit time in `FlightRoot.take_enemy_fire`, which also drops friendly fire | "Low and limited chance of shooting the player when the ship is not currently in view" | — |
| Chase carry (cheat) | `FlightModel.frame_velocity`: a carried-frame velocity that moves the ship but is not its velocity, so it never turns the nose and assist never fights it. Lent only to a ship the *player went after* (shot or locked), that the player is nose-on to, closing on and throttling toward, within `chase_assist_range`; never a friendly | "Applied velocity equal to the player's… should not turn to this velocity… only when close". First cut triggered on any interaction and dragged enemies away from wingmen whenever the player coasted in orbit (scenario check: 0 damage, 3/3 runs) | if a chase feels rubber-banded — lower `chase_assist_strength` |
| Interaction markers | A HUD overlay (`InteractionMarkers`), not world sprites: fixed screen size, clamped to the screen edge with distance when off-screen. Spray-paint look drawn procedurally (wobbly double ring, ticks, drip, speckle), seeded per ship. Tweened in from 2× / alpha 0. Red hostile, orange wary, green friendly, yellow harmless (satellite, gunless, or aggression ≤ 0.05) | "Graffiti-like target… see them even when far… animate in" | when real graffiti art lands — swap `_draw_graffiti` for a texture |
| Fly as | `FlightRoot.fly_as(size, weapon, style)` spawns a pilot vessel beside the player and takes control; the AI hunts whichever hull the player flies. Handing it back frees it | "Play as other ships… way bigger and way smaller… and the weapons they use" | — |
| Hit explosions | Non-lethal hits explode on the hull: heavy (≥ 18% max HP) always, light ones by `hit_explosion_chance`, rate-limited per ship, player and AI alike | "Explosions on ships should not only be reserved for death" | — |
| Scattered beams — root causes | (1) A held beam re-aimed every frame along the **camera's** centre line while it spawned aimed at the gunsight — flicker between two aims, and AI beams followed the player's camera. (2) Guns read off the drawn hull, which squashes and springs on hits. (3) Canted/side guns clamped to a 12° cone fired off along their barrels (measured p95 ≈ 108° on a 35-gun hull). Fixes: beams aim at the gunsight; guns use the steady flight frame; full convergence by default (`muzzle_convergence_deg` 180) and side/rear guns (> 100° off the nose) hold fire. All four kept as toggles to reproduce. Also: beams never damaged AI ships (their collider hit the StaticBody branch first) | Found and confirmed with the bug hunt: planted-bug baseline 8.95–25° average miss → 0.00° fixed | — |
| Bug-hunt rule | `BugHunt`: suspects (getter/setter/alt) × a fixed stimulus (hold fire, weave, fake hits) × metrics (per-shot deviation from a perfect gun's line, or any `record()` channel). A hunt = baseline, each suspect flipped alone, baseline again; ranked by metric change. 🐞 chip panel; CSV log | "Add an option to turn off different variables and record and compare results so a source of a bug is found" | — |
| Planet looks | `Planet.SURFACE_STYLES`: Painted, Textured bump, Toon (cel bands, tinted shade, rim, halftone — adapted from UltimateToonSource, self-contained). Picker in Settings → Render, persisted as `Session.planet_surface_style`, so a flight *starts* with it. A deleted shader falls back to Painted | The earlier batch only had a 2-way debug toggle; ask was "alternative ways to make planets… an option to start with a certain type" | — |
| Nebula sky | `NebulaSky` bakes a procedural panorama (domain-warped gas, galactic band with dust lanes, three star layers) once on the GPU into a texture, then uses it as the environment sky: one texture lookup per background pixel. Palette-driven, rebaked on palette change, off → flat colour. Half resolution on mobile | "Background space to look way more beautiful" without a per-frame noise bill | if it competes with the ships for attention — `cloud_amount` in the shader |


## Follow-up (same day): fairness, performance, respawn, popups

| Decision | Choice | Why | Revisit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Off-screen rule | Replaces the hit-time dice (15% / 2 per 10 s). Each AI ship may *fire at the player* `offscreen_shots` (2) times per `offscreen_window` (30) s, a clock that only runs while it is out of view ([method AiShip.fire_gate], asked by [SystemsSim] once per firing frame). Ship-vs-ship fire is never held; the dice fallback asks at the hit | "A ship can only ever shoot at the player twice every 30 seconds if it is not in view, timer only counts when it is not in view" | — |
| Crooked autocannon rounds | A tracer was drawn along its *barrel*; with full convergence a canted gun's shot flies to the sight at an angle to the barrel, so it flew visibly sideways. Tracers are now built along their flight direction (hull scale kept) | Measured by the check: worst drawn-axis vs path 0.00° from a gun turned 30° | — |
| Camera swinging under fire | Every enemy shot anywhere called `Juice.kick` + `Juice.shake`, and every hit anywhere shook it twice (impact + registry). Only the player's own guns kick/shake now; world hits, deaths and bumps are scaled by `AddonVfx.camera_falloff` | "Camera turns a lot when player is being shot why" | — |
| Performance | Whole-game enemy shot cap `max_enemy_shots` (160, per shot, not per volley); tracer pool per rig (up to its 220 cap) with shared materials; cosmetic effects skipped when out of view or far (`cull_offscreen_fx`: addon scenes, registry blasts, Juice bursts/lights/rings, per-hit impact stacks); explosions capped (`max_live_explosions`); registry explosion voices capped at 6; boresight ray cast once per frame; AI obstacle probes 8×/s instead of every tick; physics scenery query walks occupied cells when the neighbourhood is bigger | The profiler captures: `_spawn_shot`/`_launch`, VFX explosions/omni lights, `PhysWorld._query_scenery`, `AiBehaviour.update`, the audio thread | if the frame is still heavy — `max_enemy_shots` first |
| "Don't draw what is behind the player" | Rendering already frustum-culls; the cost was the *simulation* of effects nobody sees, which is now skipped | — | — |
| Fly-as ships dying | A flown ship took full enemy damage on an enemy's hit points. It now has the player's hull (×√size) and takes hits by the player's rules ([method FlightRoot.pilot_damage]: god mode, respawn grace, no friendly fire, `enemy_shot_damage_scale`) | "Sometimes just goes back to OG player ship after being hit" | — |
| Respawn | One tail for every respawn ([method FlightRoot._after_respawn]): popup hidden, alive, the view from before the death camera (it used to stay in FREE and even get saved), `respawn_grace` (4 s, hull blinks). The popup's button called the rebuild without any of that, and the popup could appear *after* a quick R | "Clicking does not remove the popup… not default view… dies immediately after respawning" | — |
| Juice settings not saving | They were read only when the first effect played; changing a dial before that saved every other dial at its default. `JuiceSettings.ensure_loaded()` runs first in every setter/getter and in the flight root. `juice_smoke` also used to reset the file this way; it now restores it byte for byte | "I think juice settings do not save" | — |
| Popups | [PopupSkin]: the mobile HUD profile's colours and fonts, a thick uneven sticker rim with a hard offset shadow, a slight lean, paint drips; emoji stripped from popup text. Game over is a tagged "WRECKED" sticker | "Less generic with less emojis… fit mobile graffiti style" | — |
| Log census | [LogCensus] (Godot 4.5 `Logger`) groups every line by source; chip panel + `log_census_check.gd`. Fixed what it found: a rock-knock signal that errored on every contact (rocks never came loose), "lambda capture freed" timers, a Nil→String incident handler, FXAA requested on Compatibility | "Logs and debugging to run and see what logs the most" | — |
