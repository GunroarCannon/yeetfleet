# factions.md — Factions are colours

**Companion to:** `GAMEDOC.md` §11 · decided 2026-09.

Factions are colours. Each colour — red, blue, yellow, purple (green and orange later) — is one political faction that owns territory: planets and systems painted in its colour, defended by its fleets. Every faction contains the same spread of roles — police, merchants, haulers, pirates, civilians — so the colour tells you whose space you are in, and the role tells you what that ship will do. Red is the exception: it is only pirates. Each faction has a specialty that shapes how it plays — for example the blue faction is dedicated to military, so its space is the most heavily patrolled and its ships the best fighters — with the other specialties still to be decided. Ships of the same role are similar in size and build across all factions, differing only slightly in silhouette (one faction prefers skinnier ships, another shorter, squatter ones), so a merchant looks like a merchant everywhere yet is instantly told apart by colour. Roles are marked on the ship itself: police — and other key roles — carry a small icon or special design, like a face, painted on the hull, so a police ship reads as police at a glance whatever colour it wears. Memory attaches to the colour: wrong one ship and the whole faction remembers.
